Autonomous Driving Safety Policy Framework

In safety-critical autonomous mobility, vision models cannot rely solely on a softmax argmax prediction. RoadGuard AI fuses both classification confidence and physical occlusion probability into deterministic safety policies:

TIER 1 • NORMAL DETECTION

Nominal Perception

Condition: Sign is Clear AND confidence ≥ 60%.
Action: Autonomous vehicle trajectory planner executes standard speed and lane controls.

TIER 2 • CAUTION – BLOCKED

Environmental Interference

Condition: Occlusion Head flags occlusion ≥ 50%.
Action: Issue perception warning, limit maximum speed, prioritize GPS/map prior validation.

TIER 3 • UNCERTAIN

Human Verification Required

Condition: Sign Confidence < 60% OR conflicting heads.
Action: Disengage high-speed autonomy, alert driver takeover, trigger sensor fusion fail-safe.

Academic Disclaimer: RoadGuard AI is an academic machine learning research system developed for algorithmic exploration of dual-head occlusion detection. It is not certified under ISO 26262 ASIL-D for real-world vehicular actuation.

Precision vs Recall in Safety-Critical AI

In standard computer vision, overall accuracy is often celebrated. However, in autonomous driving perception:

Recall (Sensitivity): Measures "Of all truly occluded signs, how many did the system catch?"
A false negative (missing an occluded STOP sign) can result in catastrophic collision. Therefore, high recall is vital.
Precision: Measures "When the system flags an occlusion, is it genuinely blocked?"
Low precision causes "phantom cautions", eroding operator trust and causing unnecessary decelerations.

RoadGuard AI balances both metrics via joint multi-task representation learning and weighted loss optimization.
